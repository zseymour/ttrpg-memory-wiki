# Campaign-Memory Requirements from Long-Running Duet GM Practice

## Scope and interpretation

This summary answers [issue #2](https://github.com/zseymour/ttrpg-memory-wiki/issues/2) within [issue #1's specification boundary](https://github.com/zseymour/ttrpg-memory-wiki/issues/1). It treats the craft document and templates as evidence of situations a competent duet GM must handle, not as an architecture to copy. The canonical distinction is that a **memory requirement** is behavior needed for coherent play, while files, fields, indexes, and read/update workflows are storage proposals ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 15–17](../CONTEXT.md#L15-L17)).

Classifications below mean:

- **Invariant** — must always hold for campaign memory to remain trustworthy.
- **Required capability** — behavior the subsystem must provide when the relevant producer or consumer invokes it.
- **Optional technique support** — interoperability with a GM framework that is useful in some campaigns but is not a universal memory primitive ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 143–145](../CONTEXT.md#L143-L145)).

The sources use “secret” both for undisclosed material and for Sly Flourish's portable clue/fact prep list. This summary does not use **secret** as a domain status. It uses the canonical terms **preparation**, **established truth**, **open question**, and **unrevealed material** ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 19–25](../CONTEXT.md#L19-L25), [`CONTEXT.md` lines 35–45](../CONTEXT.md#L35-L45)); “10 Secrets/Clues” appears only as the name of an optional source technique.

## Requirement families

### 1. Durable information, provenance, and scoped uncertainty — **Invariant**

- **Observed practice / evidence.** Long-campaign notes cover NPC identity, voice, motivation, status, relationships and last-seen state; faction goals and clocks; session and offscreen timelines; recurring rulings; unpaid hooks; and unused prepared clues ([craft lines 758–767](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L758-L767)). The advisory timeline records in-world dates, events, clues, clock changes, consequential choices, player feedback, and the cliffhanger ([state template lines 227–260](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/state-tracking-templates.md#L227-L260)); recurring rulings are retained specifically to preserve consistency ([state template lines 310–333](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/state-tracking-templates.md#L310-L333)).
- **Derived memory requirement.** Preserve information beyond immediate play when future consequence, continuity, perspective, player salience, preparation dependencies, or explicit promotion make it durable—not every evocative or incidental narration ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 131–137](../CONTEXT.md#L131-L137)). Durable coverage includes play history; current fictional and campaign-specific mechanical state; entities and relationships; campaign rulings; player guidance; perspectives and awareness; unrevealed material and preparation; Threads; clues and investigative questions; uncertainty; and provenance. Each durable proposition or transition must retain a traceable origin, establishment context, supported claim, and inspectable reference when one exists ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 71–73](../CONTEXT.md#L71-L73)). Uncertainty must be attached to the affected proposition and distinguish unknown, partially known, unresolved, disputed, unrecorded, and provisional states rather than inventing precision ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 155–157](../CONTEXT.md#L155-L157)).
- **Recall situations.** Resuming an interrupted scene; preparing a later session; voicing a returning NPC; applying a prior ruling; checking a date, deadline, clue, commitment, or past choice; and resolving a continuity disagreement.
- **Failure prevented.** Forgotten sessions, collapsed NPC continuity, inconsistent rulings, accidental invention, and false certainty. These are observed long-play diagnostics: forgetting produces player corrections and continuity errors, while inconsistent rulings produce different outcomes for the same situation ([craft lines 874–892](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L874-L892)).
- **Epistemic/temporal constraints.** Provenance supports a narrow claim, not everything near its source. Lack of a record is **unrecorded**, not evidence that an event did not occur. A player recap can evidence salience or perspective, but is not authoritative history; recap is an attributed consumer-generated view ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 159–161](../CONTEXT.md#L159-L161)).
- **Explicit non-requirements.** Campaign memory need not preserve the full transcript, every narrated detail, recap prose, or a specific note-taking cadence. The memory subsystem does not decide what becomes durable or perform semantic compaction; those are responsibilities of a human or autonomous **memory maintainer** using explicit memory contracts ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 163–165](../CONTEXT.md#L163-L165)).

### 2. Truth, preparation, perspective, awareness, and narrative authority — **Invariant**

- **Observed practice / evidence.** Competent play prepares situations rather than scripted outcomes, asks questions and uses player answers, and deliberately leaves blanks ([craft lines 11–25](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L11-L25)). Duet play repeatedly invites the player to author details and interpretations ([craft lines 827–831](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L827-L831)), while pre-campaign Same Page negotiation explicitly includes narrative authority and requires discussion rather than a survey answer ([craft lines 707–708](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L707-L708)). NPC continuity practice separately tracks what an NPC knows ([craft lines 186–190](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L186-L190)), and investigation practice warns that confusing who knows what can reveal unearned information or suppress information already available to the PC ([investigation lines 174–186](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/references/mystery-and-investigation.md#L174-L186)).
- **Derived memory requirement.** Keep **established truth** distinct from **preparation**, **belief**, **suspicion**, and **open question**; importing or writing preparation must not silently establish it ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 19–37](../CONTEXT.md#L19-L37)). Represent whose perspective a belief or suspicion belongs to. Track **player awareness** separately from fictional-entity belief or suspicion, and track **unrevealed material** without treating inspectability as awareness ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 39–45](../CONTEXT.md#L39-L45)). Preserve the authority context that explains whether a contribution established fiction or only proposed, believed, suspected, or questioned it ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 107–109](../CONTEXT.md#L107-L109)). Explicit campaign premises may enter as **baseline establishment**; contemporaneous imports remain in their producer-designated epistemic class ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 75–77](../CONTEXT.md#L75-L77)).
- **Recall situations.** Describing what the PC can perceive; answering what player or character already knows; voicing an NPC without impossible knowledge; improvising from preparation; accepting a player-authored detail; resolving an oracle/procedure result; and revealing previously unrevealed material.
- **Failure prevented.** Prep becoming retroactive canon, omniscient NPCs, player/character knowledge leakage, unnoticed authority overreach, premature mystery answers, and contradictions hidden by a generic “fact” status.
- **Epistemic/temporal constraints.** Speaker identity alone does not grant authority. Unrevealed established truth and unrevealed preparation remain different. A contribution can establish only what its authority permits; narrower observable facts do not automatically establish hidden cause or motive.
- **Explicit non-requirements.** Memory does not adjudicate player declarations, decide what play establishes, authorize narration, interpret oracle prompts, or choose when preparation becomes truth. The source explicitly treats oracle answers as interpretive prompts jointly resolved in fiction, not literal verdicts ([craft lines 325–345](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L325-L345)).

### 3. Fictional time, establishment order, transitions, corrections, and rewinds — **Invariant**

- **Observed practice / evidence.** Long-running play depends on calendars, deadlines, downtime, and offscreen events ([craft lines 24–25](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L24-L25), [craft lines 748–756](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L748-L756)). The advisory timeline separately carries setting dates, elapsed in-world time, offscreen events, and session logs ([state template lines 227–260](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/state-tracking-templates.md#L227-L260)). Flashbacks retroactively establish preparation but cannot undo what is already established ([craft lines 307–314](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L307-L314)); safety tools can instead rewind and erase recent content ([craft lines 680–689](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L680-L689)).
- **Derived memory requirement.** Preserve **fictional time** independently of **establishment order**, with exact, relative, or uncertain fictional dates as warranted ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 47–53](../CONTEXT.md#L47-L53)). Distinguish: (1) a **state transition**, where earlier and later states remain true at their respective fictional times; (2) a **correction**, which replaces an erroneous proposition that never belonged to fictional history; and (3) a **rewind**, which collaboratively removes or replaces previously established content and prevents it from returning ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 55–65](../CONTEXT.md#L55-L65)). Preserve inspectable transition and correction history unless a safety-driven rewind requires erasure.
- **Recall situations.** Reconstructing state at a prior date; resuming after a time skip; checking deadlines or duration; explaining why current state differs; applying a flashback; accepting a player correction; and honoring a safety rewind.
- **Failure prevented.** Session order mistaken for world chronology, a changed state treated as contradiction, an error retained as historical truth, or erased unsafe content resurfacing.
- **Epistemic/temporal constraints.** A later record can establish an earlier fictional event. Uncertain dates remain uncertain. Corrections require an explicit authoritative basis; incompatible claims without one remain a continuity conflict rather than automatic last-write-wins ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 103–105](../CONTEXT.md#L103-L105)). Safety erasure overrides historical completeness and provenance retention ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 99–101](../CONTEXT.md#L99-L101)).
- **Explicit non-requirements.** Memory does not choose update cadence, calculate time passage, decide offscreen advancement, or initiate corrections/rewinds. Offscreen advancement is a preparation activity performed by a consumer; memory preserves and records its inputs and results ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 67–69](../CONTEXT.md#L67-L69)).

### 4. Entity identity and asymmetric, evolving relationships — **Invariant**

- **Observed practice / evidence.** Returning NPCs require stable status, location, traits, last interaction, and voice continuity; relationship and who-knows-what tracking are separate concerns ([craft lines 162–190](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L162-L190)). Duet campaigns rely on fewer but deeper recurring NPCs, companion goals that may diverge from the PC's, and relationship-heavy play ([craft lines 817–836](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L817-L836)). The advisory NPC template also combines role, faction, status, last-seen state, traits, wants, knowledge, relationships, and interaction history ([state template lines 124–153](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/state-tracking-templates.md#L124-L153)).
- **Derived memory requirement.** Maintain **entity identity** independently of display name, title, disguise, or role; keep established equivalence distinct from provisional links and actor beliefs/suspicions about identity ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 111–113](../CONTEXT.md#L111-L113)). Preserve relationships as multifaceted, directional, perspective-grounded, and time-varying histories of interactions and commitments; labels such as ally or rival are summaries, not the relationship itself ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 115–117](../CONTEXT.md#L115-L117)).
- **Recall situations.** Voicing or reintroducing an NPC; interpreting a disguise or alias; determining what one party knows, owes, wants, fears, or believes about another; and portraying a relationship after intervening events.
- **Failure prevented.** Duplicate or merged identities, broken disguises, symmetric “friend/enemy” assumptions, stale disposition labels, impossible knowledge, and NPC voice or motive collapse.
- **Epistemic/temporal constraints.** “A believes B is C” is not proof that B and C are identical. A's attitude toward B need not equal B's attitude toward A. Present disposition must not erase the interactions and commitments that explain it.
- **Explicit non-requirements.** Memory does not choose NPC actions, performance, voice prose, relationship arcs, or companion behavior; it supplies the bounded evidence those GM behaviors consume.

### 5. Threads and preparation dependencies — **Required capability**

- **Observed practice / evidence.** Mythic uses Threads and Characters to ground random events in accumulated fiction ([craft lines 336–345](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L336-L345)); the duet loop maintains and updates Threads and Characters ([craft lines 839–845](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L839-L845)). The advisory state lists active Threads separately from planned payoff hooks ([state template lines 40–69](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/state-tracking-templates.md#L40-L69)).
- **Derived memory requirement.** Preserve a **Thread** only when an unresolved matter has been created or made salient through play and may materially affect the PC, relationships, or campaign. Keep unused hooks and planned payoffs as preparation until they enter play ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 119–121](../CONTEXT.md#L119-L121)). Retain a Thread's relevant origin, current pressure/state, linked entities/events, player salience where known, and resolution or abandonment without requiring any one framework's representation.
- **Recall situations.** Session preparation, resuming a cliffhanger, responding to a relevant PC choice, reviewing unresolved commitments, or supplying candidates to an optional oracle.
- **Failure prevented.** Dropped player-salient matters, forgotten promises, prep mislabeled as an active plot, and random generation detached from campaign history.
- **Epistemic/temporal constraints.** A Thread can contain established events, unresolved questions, perspectives, and preparation without collapsing those statuses. “Unresolved” does not imply a pre-existing hidden answer.
- **Explicit non-requirements.** Memory does not select, prioritize, advance, close, or pay off Threads; script plot outcomes; or require Mythic's Chaos/Tension loop.

### 6. Clues and fixed, emergent, or hybrid investigations — **Invariant and required capability**

- **Observed practice / evidence.** Investigation practice distinguishes structural from background revelations, tracks clues as found, missed, or not yet encountered, and uses proactive delivery when critical paths stall ([investigation lines 30–58](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/references/mystery-and-investigation.md#L30-L58)). Clues can be static, floating, flexible, reactive, dynamic, or proactive, so placement and discovery are not intrinsic to the clue itself ([investigation lines 17–28](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/references/mystery-and-investigation.md#L17-L28)). Some mysteries begin with no fixed solution and authorize a later procedure to establish the answer ([investigation lines 117–136](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/references/mystery-and-investigation.md#L117-L136)).
- **Derived memory requirement.** Represent a **clue** independently of whether it is prepared, available, discovered, interpreted, unavailable, redeployable, or discarded ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 123–125](../CONTEXT.md#L123-L125)). Preserve the clue's narrow content, provenance, investigative question links, availability/discovery history, and each actor's awareness or interpretation without treating the clue as its conclusion. Mark an investigative question's resolution mode as **fixed** (an answer is already established), **emergent** (an authorized procedure will establish it), or **hybrid** (established constraints bound an otherwise open answer) ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 127–129](../CONTEXT.md#L127-L129)).
- **Recall situations.** Preparing or running an investigation scene; determining what is available here; checking which clues the player/PC has received; recapping evidence when stuck; testing a theory against known constraints; and invoking the campaign's authorized resolution procedure.
- **Failure prevented.** Critical clue loss, re-awarding a discovered clue as new, accidental revelation of unrevealed material, conflating clue with conclusion, silently fixing an emergent solution, or rewriting a fixed answer to fit a theory.
- **Epistemic/temporal constraints.** Discovery establishes awareness of the clue, not necessarily its truth or the player's interpretation. A prepared clue may remain provisional. In emergent play, the solution remains an open question until the authorized outcome establishes it; in hybrid play, that outcome cannot violate established constraints.
- **Explicit non-requirements.** Memory does not choose clue redundancy, move clues, decide when a proactive clue appears, evaluate theory quality, grant information, roll, adjudicate, or narrate a solve. Three-Clue, node-graph, Gumshoe, and Brindlewood procedures are GM behavior/frameworks, not universal storage structure ([craft lines 373–416](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L373-L416)).

### 7. Campaign-specific mechanical state versus the system corpus — **Invariant**

- **Observed practice / evidence.** The advisory PC template mixes campaign-instance values—level, current resources, conditions, inventory, abilities, bonds, goals, and reputation ([state template lines 74–119](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/state-tracking-templates.md#L74-L119))—while the rulings template records recurring campaign-specific mechanical and world rulings ([state template lines 310–333](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/state-tracking-templates.md#L310-L333)).
- **Derived memory requirement.** Preserve **mechanical state** as campaign-specific configuration, resources, inventory, conditions, progression, active effects, and their transitions. Preserve campaign rulings and the provenance needed to apply them consistently. Keep the complete **system corpus**—general rules, playbooks, equipment and ability definitions, stat blocks, bestiaries, and procedures—outside campaign memory, composing it with memory at recall time ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 79–81](../CONTEXT.md#L79-L81), [`CONTEXT.md` lines 151–153](../CONTEXT.md#L151-L153)).
- **Recall situations.** Resuming a scene or combat, checking a resource or effect at a prior/current time, leveling/progression, applying a recurring house ruling, or retrieving the relevant campaign state alongside external rule references.
- **Failure prevented.** Stale HP/resources/conditions, unexplained mechanical changes, inconsistent campaign rulings, and copying the entire rules corpus into each campaign.
- **Epistemic/temporal constraints.** Current values derive from authoritative baseline and transitions; disputed values remain conflicts. A campaign ruling can differ from the external corpus and must remain attributable rather than silently rewriting it.
- **Explicit non-requirements.** Memory does not contain the complete rules corpus, parse every rules format, adjudicate mechanics, choose a ruling, or narrate a mechanical outcome.

### 8. Player guidance, safety boundaries, and safety rewinds — **Invariant and required capability**

- **Observed practice / evidence.** Session zero collaboratively establishes tone and inspiration ([session-zero template lines 1–25](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/session-zero-template.md#L1-L25)), content lines and veils ([session-zero template lines 29–62](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/session-zero-template.md#L29-L62)), safety controls including rewind/skip/pause ([session-zero template lines 66–76](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/session-zero-template.md#L66-L76)), engagement preferences ([session-zero template lines 133–158](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/session-zero-template.md#L133-L158)), and duet-specific intensity, companion, romance, and between-session preferences ([session-zero template lines 162–173](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/session-zero-template.md#L162-L173)). Craft practice treats lines/veils as living and X-Card/Script Change as edits or erasure, not ordinary continuity events ([craft lines 673–689](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L673-L689)).
- **Derived memory requirement.** Preserve **player guidance** with its kind and scope: safety boundary, explicit preference, current wish, observed affinity, or shared premise. Preserve guidance only while future decisions depend on it, except active safety boundaries, which are mandatory in every recall situation ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 139–141](../CONTEXT.md#L139-L141)). Allow guidance to be revised. Enforce safety boundaries over preparation, retrieval, and historical completeness; erasure requests must remove affected material rather than keep an inspectable hidden copy ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 99–101](../CONTEXT.md#L99-L101)).
- **Recall situations.** Every bounded recall; campaign/session preparation; framing intense, romantic, horror, or companion content; adapting engagement mix; and processing pause, skip, correction, or rewind requests.
- **Failure prevented.** Crossing a line, narrating veiled detail, treating a temporary wish as a permanent taste, flattening nuanced guidance into one preference, or resurfacing erased content through history, provenance, indexes, recaps, or preparation.
- **Epistemic/temporal constraints.** Player guidance is attributed and revisable, not fictional truth. An observed affinity is weaker than an explicit preference. Current safety boundaries override older guidance and inferred preferences.
- **Explicit non-requirements.** Memory does not conduct session zero, prompt safety check-ins, infer preferences, decide content, generate recap prose, or perform safety-tool facilitation; it stores and retrieves the resulting constraints and honors explicit erasure operations.

### 9. Situation-driven bounded recall and Recall gaps — **Invariant and required capability**

- **Observed practice / evidence.** The craft calls for checking relevant NPC, knowledge, downtime, timeline, and rulings information across sessions ([craft lines 186–190](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L186-L190), [craft lines 758–767](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L758-L767)). The template's blanket instruction to read all files and cross-reference records is a proposed workflow for achieving continuity ([state template lines 391–399](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/state-tracking-templates.md#L391-L399)), while an investigation stuck-state specifically benefits from recalling the clues already found ([investigation lines 198–208](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/references/mystery-and-investigation.md#L198-L208)).
- **Derived memory requirement.** Retrieval must be driven by a concrete **recall situation**, not a file read or global dump ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 83–85](../CONTEXT.md#L83-L85)). Return the smallest inspectable **bounded context** that preserves relevant continuity, constraints, uncertainty, and provenance, with references for deeper inspection ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 87–89](../CONTEXT.md#L87-L89)). Never omit recall-critical safety constraints, corrections/rewinds, relevant current state and commitments, epistemic status, or material uncertainty/conflict ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 91–93](../CONTEXT.md#L91-L93)). If those cannot be supplied within available context or confidence, return an explicit **Recall gap** rather than an authoritative-looking incomplete result ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 95–97](../CONTEXT.md#L95-L97)).
- **Recall situations.** At minimum: resume a scene; voice an NPC; adjudicate under precedent; prepare play; review an investigation; advance time; generate an attributed recap; inspect a contradiction; or apply a correction/rewind.
- **Failure prevented.** Context overflow from full-memory dumps, provenance-free summaries, confident omission, stale continuity, unsafe retrieval, or consumers acting as though missing critical context were complete.
- **Epistemic/temporal constraints.** Bounded recall must preserve distinctions among established truth, preparation, perspective, awareness, uncertainty, and conflict. Relevance cannot override safety or erase the temporal/provenance basis needed to interpret state.
- **Explicit non-requirements.** Retrieval is not a mandate to read every physical file, return all campaign memory, decide what the GM should do next, generate recap prose, or perform semantic compaction.

### 10. Memory-failure boundary — **Invariant**

- **Observed practice / evidence.** The craft's diagnostic table includes both continuity failures—forgotten sessions, contradictory world logic, NPC voice collapse, and inconsistent rulings—and GM-behavior failures such as railroading, over-narration, fudging, weak consequences, over/under-prep, and making the GM the protagonist ([craft lines 874–896](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L874-L896)).
- **Derived memory requirement.** A **memory failure** occurs when campaign memory cannot preserve, distinguish, reconcile, or retrieve durable information. Narration, pacing, adjudication, encounter design, agency, preparation quality, and unwanted story outcomes remain GM failures even if better recall might assist ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 147–149](../CONTEXT.md#L147-L149)). Evaluate memory against continuity, epistemic separation, temporal evolution, provenance/conflict handling, safety, and relevant bounded recall—not story quality.
- **Recall situations.** Incident diagnosis; contradiction resolution; retrieval evaluation; and acceptance testing against a known campaign history.
- **Failure prevented.** Expanding the subsystem into an autonomous GM, blaming memory for every poor play outcome, or masking real memory defects as subjective narration problems.
- **Epistemic/temporal constraints.** Diagnose the failed contract: missing durable input, misclassified status, unreconciled conflict, lost transition, unsafe retention, or insufficient recall. Better output quality alone does not prove memory correctness.
- **Explicit non-requirements.** Campaign memory does not decide update cadence, offscreen advancement, recap wording, semantic maintenance/compaction, adjudication, pacing, encounter balance, or narration.

### 11. Optional GM-framework interoperability — **Optional technique support**

- **Observed practice / evidence.** The sources offer multiple substitutable reasoning/preparation vocabularies: Fronts with threats, dark futures, and stakes questions ([craft lines 478–485](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L478-L485)); visible progress/faction clocks ([craft lines 511–514](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L511-L514)); Mythic Threads/Characters and oracle state ([craft lines 336–345](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L336-L345)); node graphs and clue-placement techniques ([investigation lines 62–87](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/references/mystery-and-investigation.md#L62-L87)); campaign acts and escalation patterns ([craft lines 734–753](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L734-L753)); and the “10 Secrets/Clues” preparation list ([craft lines 44–59](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L44-L59)).
- **Derived memory requirement.** Campaign memory may preserve framework-native artifacts and compose them with core concepts—preparation, Threads, clues, investigative questions, entities, mechanical state, provenance—without promoting Front, clock, act, node, oracle state, or a “10 Secrets/Clues” item into a universal primitive ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 143–145](../CONTEXT.md#L143-L145)). Framework artifacts still obey core epistemic, temporal, authority, safety, and provenance invariants.
- **Recall situations.** A consumer explicitly asks to prepare or run play using the selected framework, inspect its current artifacts, or relate them to established campaign information.
- **Failure prevented.** Lock-in to one GM school, semantic loss when a campaign uses a framework, a prepared dark future treated as established destiny, or an optional clock mistaken for universal time/state machinery.
- **Epistemic/temporal constraints.** Framework labels never override core status: a Front's dark future is preparation; a stakes question remains open; a clock tick becomes established only through an authorized outcome; a node is an organizational aid; an oracle result is not truth until authorized establishment.
- **Explicit non-requirements.** The subsystem need not run oracle loops, advance clocks or factions, enforce clue counts, author Fronts, structure campaigns into acts, choose framework cadence, or require any framework at all.

## Dedicated storage-layout non-requirements

The advisory templates propose eight files and a `campaign/` directory ([state template lines 375–387](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/state-tracking-templates.md#L375-L387)). That layout is evidence that the information matters to practice, not evidence that those physical boundaries are correct. In particular:

- No named file (`campaign-state.md`, `pc.md`, `npcs.md`, `factions.md`, `timeline.md`, `secrets.md`, `rulings.md`, or `last-session-recap.md`) is required.
- No template heading, field, two-column checklist, matrix, append-only log, Markdown shape, directory, or one-record-per-NPC/faction convention is required. The sources themselves note that Obsidian, World Anvil, Notion, or plain Markdown can support the practice and that discipline matters more than tool choice ([craft lines 758–767](the-craft-of-game-mastering.md#L758-L767)).
- “Read all files at session start,” “update during play,” and “close the loop at session end” are agentic/human maintenance procedures, not memory-subsystem behavior ([state template lines 391–399](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/state-tracking-templates.md#L391-L399)). The subsystem exposes explicit represent/validate/store/reconcile/retrieve contracts; it does not decide when a maintainer invokes them ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 11–13](../CONTEXT.md#L11-L13)).
- The source template's `secrets.md` is not a required confidentiality partition, truth class, or universal object. It is a proposed home for an optional Sly Flourish clue/fact preparation technique ([state template lines 264–305](gm-duet-skill/gm-duet/assets/state-tracking-templates.md#L264-L305)).
- A last-session recap file is not authoritative history and does not make recap generation a memory responsibility. Recap remains an attributed consumer-generated view ([`CONTEXT.md` lines 159–161](../CONTEXT.md#L159-L161)).
- Derived search/index structures may exist, but issue #1 requires them to be rebuildable; the authoritative campaign memory remains local-first, provider-neutral, inspectable, and directly editable. This research does not select a schema, database, index, serialization, provider, or implementation.

## Coverage of issue #2's question

- **Durable information:** families 1 and 4–8 identify what persists and why.
- **Temporal behavior:** family 3 separates fictional time, establishment order, transitions, corrections, and rewinds.
- **Epistemic boundaries:** families 1–3 and 6 distinguish truth, preparation, perspectives, awareness, authority, provenance, uncertainty, and investigation resolution modes.
- **Recall situations:** every family names concrete bounded-recall consumers; family 9 defines their common contract and Recall gaps.
- **Failure cases:** every family states the failure prevented; family 10 defines the boundary between memory and GM failure.
- **Behavior versus layout:** the requirement families state behavioral contracts; the dedicated final section rejects the templates' proposed files, fields, and workflows as requirements.

# Session Zero Template

A checklist for the conversation that happens before play begins. Adapt to the system, the player, and the tone you're aiming for. Skip nothing critical; pick and choose the rest.

Copy this template into a working document or `campaign-state.md` and fill it in *with* the player. The collaboration is the point — don't dictate.

## Section 3: Safety tools in use

Tell the player explicitly:

- **X-card:** "Type 'X' or 'pause' at any point and we stop. No explanation needed. We rewind, fast-forward, or skip past whatever's happening."
- **Open Door:** "You can leave the session at any time, no penalty, no reason needed."
- **Check-ins:** "I'll occasionally pause and ask 'how are you feeling about this scene?' — out of character. Honest answers help."

Optional additions:
- **Script Change:** Rewind / Fast-Forward / Pause / Resume.
- **Stars and Wishes:** End-of-session wrap.

## Templates for quick session zero

### One-shot (minimal)
Tone? Lines/veils? PC concept and one motivation? Safety tools acknowledged? Strong start? Go.

### Short campaign (3-10 sessions)
+ Active front + stakes questions + character backstory hooks + 2-3 NPCs from backstory.

### Long campaign (10+ sessions)
Full session zero. Take 1-2 hours. Get it right.

---

## A note on session zero

It's not a chore. It's a creative exercise. Both parties are pitching ideas, hearing them back, refining. The campaign is born here.

If session zero feels boring, it probably means you're treating it as a checklist rather than a conversation. Get curious. Ask follow-ups. The best campaigns come from session zeros where both parties walked away energized.

# State Tracking Templates

Copy these templates into a `campaign/` directory at the start of a new campaign. Update them religiously — they are your memory across sessions.

The discipline rule: **read these files at session start before describing anything; update them at session end before closing.**

---

## rulings.md

Ad-hoc rulings made during play. So you stay consistent.

```markdown
# Rulings

Append every ruling that will recur in future play. Read at session start.

## Mechanical rulings
- Torches last 6 in-game turns (10 minutes each).
- Two-weapon fighting: bonus action attack, no ability mod to damage.
- Stress-induced trembling adds disadvantage to fine-motor checks.
- Healing potions: bonus action to drink one yourself; full action to administer to another.
- Crit on natural 20 = max damage + roll, not double-roll.

## World rulings
- Magic missile cannot be deflected by ordinary armor.
- Holy water deals 2d6 to undead in this setting (not the standard amount).
- Picking pockets in this city: DC 15 base; +5 if target is alert.

## Custom procedures
- The PC can spend Inspiration to declare a flashback (Blades-style retroactive setup).
- Long rests in dungeons require a "secure rest" check; otherwise interrupted.

## Etc.
```

## Discipline rules

1. **Read all files at session start.** Confirm against them, not your memory.
2. **Update during play** when something changes (new NPC met, new ruling made, faction status changed).
3. **Close the loop at session end.** Update timeline, last-session-recap, advance offscreen factions, persist state.
4. **Don't invent contradictions.** If the player remembers something differently and the file confirms their memory, accept the correction.
5. **Cross-reference.** When voicing a returning NPC, check `npcs.md`. When mentioning a faction, check `factions.md`. When making a ruling, check `rulings.md`.

This is the discipline. It's tedious but it's what makes long-form play work.
